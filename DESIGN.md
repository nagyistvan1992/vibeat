## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, tactile editorial aesthetic tailored for culinary arts and immersive digital recipe curation. It balances the literary authority of heritage culinary journals with the frictionless utility of modern digital product design. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Sun-washed linen backdrops, pristine white recipe cards, and disciplined structural framing.
- **Sensory Visual Hierarchy:** Rich, earthen accents evoking deep forest foliage, clay bakeware, and slow-simmered reductions.
- **Micro-Detailing:** Deliberate typographic scale shifts, hairline separators, and soft organic depth that elevates photography and instruction step-by-step.
- **Tone & Atmosphere:** Calm, inviting, and uncompromisingly high-craft—designed to evoke the warmth of an artisan kitchen counter bathed in morning light.

## Colors

The color palette grounds user focus through tactile, natural pigments while maintaining WCAG AAA/AA readability across display tiers.

- **Primary (`#2C3E2D` - Deep Forest Olive):** Used for focal action states, high-priority icons, dominant titles, and active tabs. Evokes culinary herbs and natural tablewares.
- **Secondary (`#C85A32` - Terracotta Amber):** Applied sparingly for interactive accents, prep-time highlights, favoriting/bookmark states, and callout badges.
- **Tertiary (`#8A6D47` - Toasted Umber):** Drives secondary metadata, subtle icon strokes, and culinary note annotations.
- **Canvas & Neutral Surfaces:**
  - Base App Canvas: `#FAF7F2` (Warm Alabaster)
  - Secondary Canvas/Layering: `#F4EFEA` (Pressed Dough)
  - Elevated Container / Recipe Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White Porcelain)
  - Structural Hairlines & Borders: `#E8E2D8` (Sand Mortar)
  - High-Emphasis Text: `#1C201C` (Charred Herb)
  - Medium-Emphasis Text: `#575B53` (Olive Pewter)
  - Low-Emphasis/Muted Text: `#82867E` (Stone Sage)

## Typography

The typographic architecture establishes deliberate contrast between culinary storytelling and utilitarian step execution.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the high-end editorial tone of a published hardcover volume. Italic variants are reserved for subtle recipe origin notes, regional sub-headers, and quotes from culinary creators.
- **Interface & Ingredients (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its crisp geometric forms, clear numerical legibility, and high readability in dense lists, metric measurements, and timer states.
- **Instructional Body Copy:** Maintain `body-lg` (18px) with generous line heights (`28px`) for step-by-step cooking directions to ensure ease of reading from counter distance.
- **Labels & Badges:** Rendered in uppercase or small-caps with enhanced tracking (`0.04em`–`0.06em`) to ensure instant categorization on dynamic screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an asymmetric 12-column responsive fluid grid, prioritizing large-format editorial imagery and reading ergonomics.

- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1380px`, `margin` of `3rem`, and `gutter` of `1.5rem`. Recipe displays favor a balanced two-column format (sticky ingredient checklist on the left, visual step narrative on the right).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `margin-tablet` of `2rem` and `gutter` of `1rem`. Hero imagery transitions to full-bleed headers.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column grid with `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`. Cooking controls dock cleanly to a thumb-accessible bottom action sheet.
- **Rhythm:** Use `space-md` for standard component internals and `space-xl` to `space-xxl` between major editorial chapters and visual breakouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on physical paper and stone layering rather than cold digital drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Layer (`#FAF7F2`):* Canvas substrate.
  - *Secondary Wells (`#F4EFEA`):* Inset panels, ingredient prep areas, and interactive filtering sidebars.
  - *Hero Floating Plates (`#FFFFFF`):* Recipe overview cards, step instructions, and modal cards.
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Every card and boundary is framed with a hairline border (`1px solid #E8E2D8`), keeping components grounded without visual heaviness.
- **Warm Ambient Shadows:**
  - *Level 1 (Default Recipe Card):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(44, 62, 45, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.03)`
  - *Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive Card):* `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(44, 62, 45, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.04)`
  - *Level 3 (Modal Overlay / Floating Timer):* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(28, 32, 28, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(44, 62, 45, 0.04)`

## Shapes

The shape system blends the structure of printed stationery with soft, touchable geometry.

- **Base Corner Radius (`roundedness: 2` / `0.5rem`):** Applied to functional inputs, ingredient quantity pickers, and nested control chips.
- **Cards & Floating Panels (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`):** Used for recipe cards, image frames, and modular culinary tiles.
- **Pill Shapes (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for metadata badges (cooking time, dietary tags), filter pills, and circular floating action buttons.
- **Image Treatment:** Photography carries a consistent `rounded-lg` clip accompanied by an inset `1px` translucent border (`rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`) to anchor vibrant food imagery into light surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#2C3E2D` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-md` (0.5rem), `12px 24px` padding. On hover: shifts slightly warmer (`#374D38`) with Level 2 shadow.
- **Secondary (Terracotta Accent):** Solid `#C85A32` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for primary cooking actions ("Start Cook Mode", "Add to Collection").
- **Outline / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1px solid #E8E2D8` border, `#2C3E2D` text. On hover: `#F4EFEA` background and `#2C3E2D` border.

### Badges & Dietary Tags
- Built as full pill-shaped tokens with subtle tint fills:
  - *Dietary (Vegan, Gluten-Free):* Background `rgba(44, 62, 45, 0.08)`, text `#2C3E2D`.
  - *Cooking Metric (Time, Heat, Difficulty):* Background `rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.10)`, text `#C85A32`.
  - *Nutritional (Calories, Macros):* Background `#F4EFEA`, border `1px solid #E8E2D8`, text `#575B53`.
- Sizing: `4px 10px` padding, typography set to `label-md` or `label-sm`.

### Recipe Cards
- Surface `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a `1px solid #E8E2D8` perimeter, with `rounded-lg` corners.
- Fixed aspect-ratio image header (4:3 or 16:10) topped with pill badges in the top corners.
- Content body features a Playfair Display headline, preparation time badge, author attribution, and rating stars in `#C85A32`.

### Ingredient Checklists
- Items are separated by soft `#E8E2D8` hairline divides.
- Checkboxes: Custom rounded squares (`6px` radius) with `1.5px solid #82867E`. Checked state transitions to solid `#2C3E2D` with an off-white `#FAF7F2` checkmark icon. Checked text shifts to strike-through with `#82867E` opacity.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Inset container `#F4EFEA` or solid `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E8E2D8`.
- Focus state: `1.5px solid #2C3E2D` with an outer soft halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(44, 62, 45, 0.12)`).
- Typography set to `body-md` in `#1C201C`, placeholder in `#82867E`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Prep & Cook Metric Strip:** A horizontal trio-block displaying Prep Time, Cook Time, and Servings with bold sans-serif numerals and muted label captions.
- **Cook Mode Overlay Bar:** A floating bottom bar (`#FFFFFF` surface, Level 3 elevation, pill-shaped action triggers) featuring step incrementation buttons, active timer indicators, and high-contrast voice command triggers.
